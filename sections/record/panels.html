    The Fee Card

    What the coin pays, and to whom — verified against pump.fun's own published fee documentation (fees page, last updated 16 November 2026). Rates are pump.fun's schedule and can change on their word; the split below is the town's covenant and does not change.

    On the bonding curveCreator fee 0.300% of every trade, paid in SOL
    After graduationCreator fees continue on the canonical PumpSwap pool, tiered by market cap — 0.300% in the lowest band, peaking at 0.950%, stepping down to 0.050% at the largest caps. Only the canonical pool pays a creator fee; other pools pay 0%.
    Liquidity at migrationThe bonding curve's liquidity migrates to PumpSwap and the LP tokens are burnt — not locked, burnt. Nobody can withdraw the migrated liquidity.
    The splitCreator fees split 80 / 20 — eighty percent is the founder's income, his pay for building and running the project, disclosed in the open. Twenty percent goes to the Golden Wall, off the top, forever.

    The founder's only income from this project is the disclosed creator fees. Nothing else is skimmed, and no return is promised to anybody.

    The law of the Golden Wall, published as record. It rides in every launch post, word for word.

    20% of creator fees buys physical 1g gold bars stored in a named third-party bullion vault. Each brick: receipt, photo, serial posted to public counter /47. The wall belongs to no one. It cannot be sold. It will be buried at Provadia or donated to a museum, by Bricklayer vote. Nothing else can happen to it. Never backing, never redeemable.

    The covenant, in brief

    The bricksSerial-numbered 1g physical gold bars, bought from a named dealer the moment the accumulated 20% share covers 1g spot plus premium, shipping and vault costs.
    CustodyAllocated storage at a named, licensed third-party bullion vault. The founder never physically holds the bars.
    The one withdrawalONE withdrawal is permitted, ever — the end ceremony — announced 7 days ahead, with dealer confirmation of dispatch.
    ProofPer brick: receipt, photo and serial posted publicly to the counter. The counter stands at 0 / 47.
    The endAt 47 bricks or the coin's first birthday, whichever comes first. The Bricklayer vote chooses only between burial at Provadia or donation to the Varna Regional Museum. The gold is never sold, never backing, never redeemable.

    The founder statutes, in brief

    GivebackNo airdrops of value to holders — announced or proportional — ever. The project's giving is structural: the Wall, the town, and the public record.
    Surprise HonorsRare, unannounced, for deeds done for the town — never for holdings, never promised, never marketed. Standard form: a brick laid into the Wall in the citizen's honor, paid from the founder's own share, the name and the deed recorded in the Day-Book.
    ReinvestmentThe site and the contract earn nothing for anybody. The founder's only income is the disclosed creator fees, split 80 / 20 as the Fee Card states. No trading for anybody. No promises sold to anybody.
